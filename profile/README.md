# Pughaus

AI products that live in your home, on your hardware.

Pughaus builds local-first AI tools: they run on your own machine and your own GPU, not in someone else's cloud.

## The name

Pug from Pugliese, the founder's surname (and our pug logo), plus *haus*: the home lab, turned into a company. It's the house our products live in.

Website: [pughaus.dev](https://pughaus.dev)
